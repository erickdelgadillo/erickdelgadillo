# I'm Erick Delgadillo-Nuño

**Oceanographer · Bioinformatician · Molecular Biologist**

I study how marine microbial communities respond to environmental change and build reproducible computational workflows to analyze biological data. My work connects microbial ecology, molecular biology, oceanography, and bioinformatics, with a particular interest in phytoplankton-bacteria interactions, coral reefs and marine metatranscriptomics.

I am currently completing a PhD in **Marine Science, Technology and Management** at the University of Vigo. Previously, I worked as a Research Technician at the Spanish Institute of Oceanography (IEO), in the A Coruña Oceanographic Center (COAC).
